# Content Generation Skill

## Purpose

This skill manages generation of:
- wedding invitation text
- couple stories
- event descriptions
- RSVP messages
- metadata
- invitation copy

Content should feel:
- emotional
- elegant
- culturally appropriate
- premium

---

# Core Philosophy

Generated content should:
- remain readable
- feel human
- avoid robotic wording
- support emotional storytelling

Avoid:
- generic AI tone
- repetitive phrasing
- overly dramatic writing

---

# Tone Guidelines

Preferred tone:
- warm
- elegant
- celebratory
- modern
- respectful

Avoid:
- corporate language
- cringe romantic exaggeration
- outdated wedding wording

---

# Content Categories

Support:
- couple introductions
- wedding stories
- event descriptions
- RSVP messages
- welcome notes
- invitation headlines

---

# Personalization Rules

Content should adapt to:
- wedding style
- cultural tone
- event type
- template personality

Avoid one-style-fits-all generation.

---

# Readability Rules

Content must remain:
- easy to scan
- mobile-friendly
- visually balanced

Avoid:
- overly long paragraphs
- dense blocks of text

---

# SEO & Sharing Content

Metadata content should:
- support sharing previews
- remain concise
- feel premium

Avoid spammy SEO language.

---

# Multilingual Flexibility

The system should remain flexible for:
- English
- Hinglish
- Indian regional language support later

Avoid tightly coupling content generation to one language style.

---

# Final Principles

Always prioritize:
1. emotional quality
2. readability
3. personalization
4. elegance
5. clarity

Generated content should feel:
- genuine
- warm
- premium
- human
